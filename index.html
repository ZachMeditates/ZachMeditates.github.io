Do you apply for me?

No. We find the jobs worth your time and get your resume and cover letter ready. You apply yourself, in your own name. We never contact employers for you.

Do I pay more if I get hired?

No. Every price is a flat fee. No commission, and no cut of your pay, ever.

How do I pay?

By PayPal or Zelle, once you decide to go ahead after the free call.

How long until my first report?

3 to 5 days after our first talk.

How often do I get new reports?

After your first report, you choose: every month or every week. Switch, pause or stop anytime.

Does it work for my field?

Yes. We read job postings against what you told us, not just your job title, so it works for nurses, designers, software testers, salespeople and more.

Who sees my information?

Only you and us, plus the services we use to do the work: secure hosting, and AI tools that read job postings and help write your documents. Your report is on a private page that opens with a one-time code sent to your email. We never send your resume or details to employers.

What about privacy?

The only personal details this site asks for are your name and email, in the sign-up form, and we use them only to reply to you. Visits are counted without cookies. We never sell your details.
